Back to Zero

It isn’t a choice, it’s a habit — the sound of pushing the calipers back to zero at the end of every shift.

Scene 01

Consumables inspection. The supplier was a student of his twenty-eight years ago, and a envelope comes across the dinner table. Sampling finds the alloy insert three hundredths of a millimetre thin — inside contract tolerance. Sign it and nobody is embarrassed.

Scene 02

Twenty-eight years back. He borrowed the school’s imported gauges, took them home, and they were stolen in the rain. He paid back 3,860 yuan, item by item, and walked back into the workshop over twelve years.

Scene 03

He writes “rejected” on the sheet. On his last day he hands a locked tin box to the student: a 1998 receipt, and a caliper worn smooth at the surname. The student pushes the reading back to 0.00.

AI short film · 45″ · 2.39:1

Awaken

The last lion was rebuilt in metal. Its heartbeat is still the drum.

Scene 01

A neon alley after rain. A dusty mechanical lion head hangs dormant on a data shrine — cold cyan light, water running off the mane, holographic dust adrift. It is waiting for a signal.

Scene 02

One orange-red point lights the pupils, and circuitry spreads like veins to the tips of the mane. The core ignites, fibre by fibre. Faded archive footage surfaces out of the signal: a cloth lion, collecting the greens.

Scene 03

It leaps to a rooftop, mane exploding into gold filament, magenta arcs wrapping the body. The dance ends, the rain stops, daylight comes. The head stands above the skyline, breathing light in and out.

    05 — Imprint Skill

    One source,
    four artefacts.

    Rules frozen into code, not nudged by hand. One Markdown file becomes four finished things — change the skin, never the text.

    EngineTypst OutputPDF · EPUB · HTML · DOCX Covers5 styles Tests87 assertions
    System
    EngineTypst. Thirty-two milliseconds a page, syntax close to Markdown, every typographic decision written as a function.
    InputOne book.yml holds trim size, skin and cover. Body text is plain Markdown; images and notes resolve by relative path.
    OutputPDF for print, EPUB 3 for stores, single-page HTML for preview, DOCX for editors. Numbers, folios and cross-references share one source.
    PlatesImages are never stretched. Side-by-side plates share a crop height. Type over image gets an automatic scrim; if contrast still fails it recomputes.
    CoversSolid, Minimal, Centered, Photo, Magazine — one field switches. No image supplied means a colour block, never an empty frame.
